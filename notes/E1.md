# E1 — VAE alignment probe (SPEC §6)

**Question:** a checkerboard prompt at 1024² — do the block edges land on
16-px multiples? Confirms the Qwen-Image-2.1 VAE 16× spatial compression
(SPEC §4, previously a secondary source) and the write-size alignment
argument.

**Date:** 2026-10-17. **Server:** 127.0.0.1:8189 (4080/16GB), UNET
`qwen_image_2.1_int8_convrot.safetensors` (8188 was down). Turbo 8-step
sampling untouched. **Arm:** t2i (`workflows/t2i_ca.json`), 1024×1024,
seed 42. Script: `experiments/e1_alignment.py` (re-analyze with
`--no-generate`); raw results `images_out/e1/e1_results.json`.

## Method

- Prompts: flat two-color (red/blue) checkerboards — "4×4" and "8×8" —
  edge to edge, pixel-art framing (flat colors, sharp edges, no
  gradient/texture/border).
- Edge detection: per row (vertical edges) / per column (horizontal
  edges), sign of R−B per pixel, edge placed at the center of the
  ambiguous transition zone; every row crosses each vertical edge once,
  so true edges accumulate ≥512/1024 votes in the position histogram;
  clusters within 6px merged (vote-weighted centroid).

## Result — 8×8 checkerboard (the clean probe): edges on the 16px grid

Expected edges: 128, 256, 384, 512, 640, 768, 896 (all 128px-multiples).

| axis | detected (px) | Δ to expected (px) | max \|Δ\| |
|---|---|---|---|
| vertical | 126.7, 255.7, 382.8, 511.1, 639.1, 767.5, 896.2 | −1.3, −0.3, −1.2, −0.9, −0.9, −0.5, +0.2 | **1.3** |
| horizontal | 127.5, 255.8, 385.2, 512.1, 640.9, 768.5, 897.2 | −0.5, −0.2, +1.2, +0.1, +0.9, +0.5, +1.2 | **1.2** |

All 14 edges recovered, every one within **1.5 px** of its 128px grid
line (mod-16 residual ≤ 1.4 px). Transition zones are thin (mostly a
single ambiguous pixel) — the VAE decodes block boundaries sharply, with
~±1.3 px of positional wobble, not blur.

**c4 (4×4 prompt):** the model ignored "4×4" and produced an 8×8-ish
messy grid (30+ color runs on one line; edges scattered, e.g. 123.8,
406.9, 715.9, 901.3). Not a VAE finding — a **prompt-fidelity** note for
E4: "N×N checkerboard" phrasing is not reliably obeyed at N=4 (N=8 was
exact). Use block-count phrasing or draw the spec differently in later
probes.

## Conclusion

- **16× VAE compression confirmed at 1024²**: 128px block edges (integer
  latent coords at 1024/16 = 64×64) come back on the 16px pixel grid to
  within ~1.5 px. SPEC §4's alignment argument holds: block boundaries
  on 16px multiples are latent-aligned and stable; off-grid boundaries
  would be smeared across latents.
- Implication for E2: at write 512 (latent 32×32) a 32-art tile is
  1 art px = 1 latent (16px blocks) — alignment-matched; at write 1024
  the same tile is 2× oversampled (still latent-aligned at 32px blocks);
  at write 256 (latent 16×16) 32-art blocks are 8px = half a latent —
  expect the model to effectively paint 16-art (SPEC §4 prediction).
- ~1.3 px wobble is far below one art pixel at any E2 write size →
  block-mode quantize (mode over the block) is the right downscale.
